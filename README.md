# PrepWise AI

> Practice job interviews with an AI that actually reads your resume.

---

## What is PrepWise AI?

PrepWise AI is an AI-powered mock interview platform. Most interview prep tools ask you the same generic questions — "Tell me about yourself", "What's your weakness?" — regardless of who you are.

PrepWise AI is different. It reads **your resume**, scores it, and then interviews you on your actual experience — your projects, your skills, the companies you've worked at. The AI listens to your answers and asks follow-up questions just like a real interviewer would.

---

## How It Works

**1. Sign in with Google**
No sign-up form. Just one click.

**2. Upload your resume**
Supports PDF and DOCX. PrepWise AI reads through your experience, skills, and projects.

**3. Get your resume scored**
Instantly see how your resume performs across six dimensions:
- ATS Compatibility
- Work Experience
- Skills Section
- Projects
- Education
- Impact & Quantification

**4. Start your mock interview**
The AI generates personalized questions based on your resume and interviews you over voice — just like a real interview call. It digs deeper based on how you answer.

**5. Review your performance**
After the session, see a full breakdown of how you did. Track improvement across multiple sessions over time.

Built with Next.js, Node.js, Google Gemini AI, and WebRTC for real-time voice interviews.
